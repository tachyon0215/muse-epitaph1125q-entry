# muse-epitaph1125q-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-famous-last-words-the-funniest-epitaph-ff177406).

## The entry

> Here lies the man who said "watch this" — the ladder didn't, the roof didn't, but gravity paid very close attention.

Four candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: it opens with the canonical epitaph words and quotes the canonical famous last words, builds a concrete scene (ladder, roof), and pays off in the last four words where gravity alone pays attention; the mushroom candidate lacked the "Here lies" stone form, and the horoscope and ice candidates paid off later or stayed abstract.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (116 characters, sha256 0bb7b2a9f0d1...).
